🗡️ Royal Nightmare

Royal Nightmare Custom Instance (iRO)
A solo, character-bound instance script for RO, set in a nightmare version of Prontera. The Nightmare Queen took the throne after the royal family fell and turned the royal banquet into a curse. Her Death Witches' dark magic turned the townspeople into monsters, and she plans to do the same to the player. Nightwing pretends to serve her while secretly helping the player break the curse and get out safely.

🌟 Features:
Solo and character-bound: One player per run, tied to the character ID. The owner can re-enter while the run is still active.
60-minute time limit: A live overhead HUD shows time left and current progress. There are chat warnings at 10, 5 and 1 minute, and the player is teleported out when time runs out.
8-hour cooldown: Starts when the reward chest is claimed and is stored as a character variable.
Entry requirement: Base Level 230 or higher.
Clean map: Non-instance NPCs on the instance map are removed when a run starts.
Lore-driven dialogue: Nightwing and the Queen have story dialogue for every stage.

📜 Stage Flow:
Horde 1: 700 of each monster spawn, and the player must kill 500 of each (Grote, Pierrotzoist, Disguiser).
Queen, round 1: 3 Death Witch MVPs.
Horde 2: 700 of each spawn again, and the player must kill 666 of each.
Queen, round 2: 5 Death Witch MVPs.
Grand finale: The final MVP (ID 21361) and 2 Death Witches. Every 15 seconds, 10 of each horde monster appear around the final MVP.
Reward chest: The player claims the rewards and is teleported back to Prontera.
It takes approximately 20-30min to complete.

📦 Rewards: 5x item 102071; 5x item 102074; 30x item 1000274; 3x item 102562 (Demi Freyja Antiquity);  5,000,000 Zeny; 100,000,000,000 Base EXP and 60,000,000,000 Job EXP.
