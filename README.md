🗡️ Royal Nightmare

Royal Nightmare Custom Instance (iRO):
A solo or party, character-bound instance script for RO, set in a nightmare version of Prontera. The Nightmare Queen took the throne after the royal family fell and turned the royal banquet into a curse. Her Death Witches' dark magic turned the townspeople into monsters, and she plans to do the same to the player. Nightwing pretends to serve her while secretly helping the player break the curse... and plotting to dethrone her for good. But Leticia is hiding more than a crown: deep in the fallen court sleeps the power of a demi-goddess, and she won't go down without using it.

🌟 Features:
Solo or party: The party leader (or solo player) opens the run at Nightwing; party members join by talking to Nightwing too, and anyone in the run can come back after dying while it is open. Only the leader talks to the NPCs inside, everyone's kills count, and every member gets the rewards.
Character-bound cooldown: each character has their own 8-hour cooldown.
60-minute time limit: A live overhead HUD shows time left and current progress. There are chat warnings at 10, 5 and 1 minute, and the player is teleported out when time runs out.
8-hour cooldown: Starts when the reward chest is claimed and is stored as a character variable.
Entry requirement: Base Level 230 or higher.
Clean map: The map's other NPCs are hidden while a run is on, and come back when it ends.
Sealed courtyard: Barricades close off the castle courtyard, so nobody wanders off into it.
Lore-driven dialogue: Nightwing, the Queen and the Goddess of Nightmare have story dialogue for every stage, with portrait pop-ups.
Instance music: The Royal Nightmare theme plays the whole time you're inside, and keeps playing through stage warps.
Spare monsters: Horde 3 scatters extra monsters around the map, so a player who dies and comes back can still finish the wave.

📜 Stage Flow:
Horde 1: 700 of each monster spawn, and the player must kill 500 of each (Grote, Pierrotzoist, Disguiser).
Queen, round 1: 3 Death Witch MVPs.
Horde 2: 700 of each monster spawn, and the player must kill 666 of each (Gan Ceann, Demigod, Tainted Wind Spirit).
Queen, round 2: 5 Death Witch MVPs.
Horde 3, wave 1 (The Kades Ambush): 10 Kades surround the player, joined by 15 Tainted Flame Spirits. Kill all of them to move on.
Nightwing's Betrayal: Nightwing reveals his plan to Leticia, and she slaps the player across Prontera.
Horde 3, wave 2 (The Queen's Beasts): Two rows of R001 Bestia and Bone Detale lead back to the fountain. Kill 50 to clear Horde 3.
Grand finale: Leticia transforms into the Goddess of Nightmare and summons the final MVP (ID 21361) with 2 Death Witches. Every 15 seconds while someone is close to her, 35 monsters spawn around them: 5 of each Horde 1 and Horde 2 monster, plus 1 Death Witch, 1 R001 Bestia, 1 Bone Detale, 1 Kades and 1 Mutated Tainted Flame Spirit.
Reward chest: The player claims the rewards and is teleported back to Prontera.
It takes approximately 20-30min to complete (if well geared).

📦 Rewards: 5x item 102071; 5x item 102074; 30x item 1000274; 3x item 102562 (Demi Freyja Antiquity); 5,000,000 Zeny; 100,000,000,000 Base EXP and 60,000,000,000 Job EXP.
